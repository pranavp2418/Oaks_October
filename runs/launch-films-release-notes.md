Three original launch films: Lintel, Portwise and Fallow. Each has a native 3840×2160 master, a 1920×1080 web edition and a selected poster baked into frame zero. Source compositions, capture helpers, original scores, licenses and technical/editorial evidence are retained in the matching project launch directories.

These are film artifacts, not a claim that all three applications have completed their release gates. Lintel footage uses its actual production workflow. Portwise and Fallow footage uses actual local builds; their production deployments remain pending. All data is synthetic, workspaces are single-user, and no customer system, authority or physical equipment is operated. Fallow's investigator is deterministic and uses the actual Rust/WASM core; no LLM or scientific-validation claim is made. Captures are edited for time, not performance benchmarks.

Choose `*-launch.mp4` for the 1080p player and `*-launch-4k.mp4` for the native master. Posters and per-film QA reports accompany these files. Music and cue accents are original local synthesis; there are no paid assets or services.
